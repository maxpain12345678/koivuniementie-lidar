# Road lidar

A gravel road measured with a trailer-mounted lidar on 18.10.2026: five runs with
RTK positioning (Unicore UM982), a LDROBOT D500 lidar scanning across the road.

https://maxpain12345678.github.io/Road_lidar/ is a 3D viewer in English, German,
French or Finnish (menu at the top right of the panel). It shows the measured
points, a ground surface from the national laser scanning, the measured points
connected into triangles, and the aerial photo as layers. Fly with WASD or the
arrow keys. The page loads about 260 MB.

Coordinates are local metres from the centre of the area. Elevations are N2000.

Data and software:
- National Land Survey of Finland: laser scanning 2023 (thinned) and orthophotos,
  CC BY 4.0 (https://www.maanmittauslaitos.fi/en/opendata-licence-cc40)
- three.js (MIT)
